FireZines

FireZines are annual(ish) project recap zines I make during my breaks between projects! I love making these to document and celebrate all the ups and downs my friends and I go through each season. I've also found that the physical booklets are great conversation starters and make for more memorable introductions!

Refactorman

Refactorman is a comic series I created about a silly superhero and a cast of silly characters. The first comic started as doodles I made while trying not to fall asleep in a mind-numbingly boring software engineering lecture. There was no plan to start a series but the characters soon took on a life of their own and somehow there are now two of these stories!

The Firekeeper

A short comic about belonging and the often thankless yet vital work of community building. Inspired by my time in Hong Kong.
